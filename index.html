Unofficial / independently produced. Not a University at Buffalo publication and not endorsed by UB, the College of Arts and Sciences, or the UB Curriculum Office. Derived automatically from the Undergraduate Catalog's own "Curricular Plan" section for each major, which the catalog itself describes as a roadmap, not a guarantee — actual requirements are governed by the official Undergraduate Catalog.
