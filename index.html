Semester-by-semester sample sequencing for a pilot set of College of Arts and Sciences majors, modeled on the School of Engineering & Applied Sciences' own advisement flowsheets. Hover a course to highlight its prerequisite/co-requisite/post-requisite relationships (same mechanism as the Engineering tool, no drawn lines); click a course for its description, and boxes marked "one of several options" for the full list of alternatives.

Unofficial / independently produced. Not a University at Buffalo publication and not endorsed by UB, the College of Arts and Sciences, or the UB Curriculum Office. Sequencing is derived from the Undergraduate Catalog's own "Curricular Plan" section (a roadmap, not a guarantee); relationship highlighting is auto-extracted, best-effort, and may be incomplete or occasionally wrong. Always confirm requirements with your academic advisor and the official Undergraduate Catalog.
